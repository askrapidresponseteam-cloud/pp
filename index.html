01 / THE QUESTION AT THE HEART OF THE BOOK

LOOKS RIGHT.
FEELS YOURS?

You can build a life that looks wonderful from the outside and still feel like a stranger inside it.

This book is for women learning to tell the difference between what they want and what they have learned to want. Through love, family, money, work and comparison, Pooja Prakash asks how we can make choices with greater honesty and intention.

FROM THE INTRODUCTION
“The point is not to choose the ‘right’ life. The point is to know when you are choosing.”

03 / CHAPTER TWO - WHOSE VOICE ARE YOU LISTENING TO?

WHOSE VOICE
IS THAT?

Some expectations become so familiar that they start sounding like your own thoughts.

Family, culture, approval and comparison can shape what you believe you should want. This book invites you to notice those voices and decide which beliefs you want to keep, question or release.

05 / THE WOMAN BEHIND THE WORDS

POOJA
PRAKASH.

Pooja writes about the questions beneath the lives we build: what we want, what we inherit, what we tolerate, and what we believe we should want.

Written with her daughter in mind, A Life You Recognise gives women space to pause, question expectations and choose with greater awareness. It offers reflection, rather than a single formula for a good life.
