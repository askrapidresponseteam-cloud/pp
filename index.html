You don’t need to have it all figured out. You just need to recognise what’s yours.

A Life You Recognise is a thoughtful and practical guide for women ready to pause, reflect and make choices that truly feel like their own. It is about more than finding yourself. It is about noticing the life you are living, understanding how you got there, and deciding what comes after.

With honesty, warmth and clarity, it explores the expectations, relationships, beliefs and everyday decisions that shape a life, and gives you the tools to choose differently. You do not have to follow the path you were given.

ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Meet
Pooja

A writer exploring the questions that sit underneath everyday life.

Pooja Prakash writes about how we make decisions, how expectations shape us, how relationships influence who we become, and how we build a life that feels genuinely our own.

She believes a meaningful life does not have to follow a particular formula. It requires attention, honesty, courage and the willingness to keep choosing.
